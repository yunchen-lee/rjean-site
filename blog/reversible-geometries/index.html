    Reversible Geometries

    本文介紹 Reversible Geometries，一個能在方盒、球體與金字塔之間變形的雙面布偶。無紡布支撐每一個幾何形狀，小型拉鍊則連接不同布層，讓一個形狀可以翻轉成另一個形狀。這個專案探索縫紉與可逆結構如何將三種不同的幾何形體結合在同一個柔軟物件中。

    This article presents Reversible Geometries, a reversible plushie that transforms between a box, a sphere, and a pyramid. Nonwoven fabric supports each geometric form, while small zippers connect the layers and allow one shape to turn into another. The project explores how sewing and reversible construction can bring three different geometries together in one soft object.

    Reversible Geometries

    My idea is to make a reversible plushie that can transform from a box to a sphere to a pyramid. To implement this idea, I studied how to make a reversible plushie that can transform from one shape to another using a zipper, and pushed it further by adding a third shape.

    Materials + Sewing

    I chose nonwoven fabric as the material because it can provide good support for the geometric shapes. I also bought some small zippers from Amazon and tried to figure out how to sew them on. Please ignore the ugly sewing, haha.

    Result — Reversible Geometries

    Surprisingly, it worked! But the zipper was too weak, so it broke after I filmed this video.

    If I could do this project again, there are two things I would try to improve:

    1. I might try linen with interlining because the nonwoven fabric is too thick, and there isn’t enough space for two shapes to fit inside one shape. For example, you can see that I need to pull the box out of the sphere before putting them both inside the pyramid.
    2. The zippers should be bigger. It is too hard to pull the shapes out and stuff them back in through a small zipper.
